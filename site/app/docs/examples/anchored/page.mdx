# Anchored

<Lead>Content that rests on top of its trigger and grows out of it.</Lead>

The page stays usable behind it.

## Popover

A button that becomes a menu. Wrap each item in `Eclose.Close` to dismiss on click.

<Example name="popover" />

## Pill

A status pill that becomes a live activity. Give the content the same background and the two read as one shape.

<Example name="pill" />

## Search field

An icon button that widens into a field. `origin="left"` keeps the icon where it is, and an `autoFocus` child takes focus on open.
